About
The gap between policy intent and real outcomes isn’t a communication problem. It’s a systems problem — and it’s solvable.

My work is about making governments deliver — building the performance systems, governance structures, and accountability frameworks that close the gap between what policy promises and what people actually experience. I’ve spent a career doing this work in both official languages, across some of Canada’s largest and most complex public sector environments.

Senior leaders bring me in when the stakes are high and the margin for error is low — when they need someone who understands how institutions actually function, not just how they’re supposed to.
